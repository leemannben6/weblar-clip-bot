Weblar Clip Bot

A personal desktop tool used by one independent clipper (Switzerland) who takes part in official clipping campaigns, where Twitch streamers pay clippers to share short highlight clips of their streams.

What it does

  1. Watches the public Twitch chat of streamers who run a clipping campaign and detects highlight moments (sudden chat activity).
  2. Creates an official Twitch clip of that moment via the Twitch API.
  3. Converts the clip to vertical format and adds a visible credit (e.g. “twitch.tv/streamer”).
  4. Uploads it as a short video to the operator's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API) and TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API), with credit to the streamer and a paid-campaign disclosure (#ad).

Only content from streamers whose campaigns explicitly allow clipping is uploaded. The tool is not sold or offered to other users.
